# Refund & Return Policy (template for the demo store)

> Starting point to edit, written for this store's products (smart LED desk lamp, wireless earbuds, phone case, ebook, ceramic mug).
> Not legal advice: check consumer-law requirements for the countries you sell to before using it for real.
> Paste the final text into DisputeShield → Settings → Refund policy.

---

**Returns & refunds — [Store name]**
Last updated: [date]

**1. 30-day returns.** You can return any physical item within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the
item price. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Electronics must include all accessories (power adapter,
cables, ear tips, charging case).

**2. How to start a return.** Email **[support email]** with your order number and the reason. We reply within
**2 business days** with the return address and instructions. Please don't send items back without contacting us first.

**3. Return shipping.** If the item arrived damaged, defective, or different from its listing, **we pay** return
shipping and send you a prepaid label. For other returns (changed your mind, wrong size or colour ordered), return
shipping is **paid by you**; we recommend a tracked service.

**4. Refund timing.** We refund to your **original payment method (PayPal)** within **5 business days** of receiving
and checking the return. You'll get an email when it's issued.

**5. Damaged, defective or not as described.** Tell us within **7 days of delivery** and include photos of the item
and packaging. We'll offer a replacement or a full refund including original shipping. You may not need to send the
item back.

**6. Items that didn't arrive.** Lamps, earbuds and mugs ship with tracking; phone cases ship by standard mail
(untracked). If tracking shows no delivery after the
estimated date, or it shows delivered but you don't have it, contact us: we'll open a claim with the carrier and send
a replacement or a full refund.

**7. Digital products (ebooks).** Because digital files can't be returned, ebooks are **non-refundable once
downloaded**. If the file is faulty, won't open, or never arrived, contact us within 14 days and we'll fix it or
refund you.

**8. Electronics (desk lamp, earbuds).** Covered by a **1-year limited warranty**. If an item stops working or
doesn't work as described, we'll first send you a short, private troubleshooting page: many problems (charging
contacts, pairing, settings) are fixed in a few minutes. If the fault remains, we repair, replace or refund it as in
section 5. Stop using any item that gets hot, sparks or smells of burning, and tell us straight away.

**9. Handmade items.** Each mug is handmade, so small variations in glaze and colour are normal and aren't a
defect. Damage in transit is always covered (see section 5).

**10. Cancellations.** You can cancel for a full refund until the order ships. Once it has shipped, the return policy
above applies.

**11. Exchanges.** We don't do direct exchanges: return the item for a refund and place a new order.

**12. Contact.** [support email] · [phone, optional] · Replies within 2 business days.

---

**Tips for editing (why it helps in disputes):**
- Keep the **time limits** (30 days, 7 days, 14 days): PayPal reviewers compare them with the dates in the case.
- Say **which items ship tracked** and keep it true (it must match each listing): it supports "item not received" defences.
- Keep the **digital-goods rule**: it is the main defence for ebook disputes.
- Keep it short and factual; this text is sent to PayPal as evidence for "not as described" disputes.
